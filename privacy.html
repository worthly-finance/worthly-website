Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how worthly ("Worthly", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the worthly mobile application.

In short: Worthly is designed as a local-first personal finance application. Your financial records are stored on your device and Worthly does not operate a server that receives or stores your financial records.

1. Information You Enter

Worthly allows you to enter and manage personal financial information, which may include:

  • Assets and cash balances
  • Investment information and values
  • Loans and outstanding balances
  • Personal debts and repayments
  • EMI information and payment dates
  • Dates, names, notes and other information you choose to enter

This information is stored locally on your device for the operation of the application. Worthly does not provide a cloud financial account for storing this information.

2. How Your Financial Data Is Stored

Worthly stores its financial records locally on your Android device. The application uses local device storage for financial records and Android secure storage for app-lock security credentials.

Worthly does not maintain a Worthly account system or a remote database containing your financial records.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Worthly is designed without the following features:

  • No Worthly user account or registration is required.
  • No access to your contacts is required.
  • No GPS or precise location access is required.
  • No advertising ID is used for advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party advertising service is integrated.
  • No third-party analytics service is integrated.
  • No third-party crash-reporting service is integrated.
  • No bank or brokerage account connection is provided.
  • Worthly does not provide financial transactions or money transfers.

4. Network and Third-Party Services

Worthly does not provide a server-side service for uploading or storing your financial records. The audited application does not contain an application-level financial-data upload mechanism, advertising service, analytics service, or third-party crash-reporting service.

Android itself may provide operating-system services such as device backup, notifications, security and application distribution. Those operating-system services are governed by the relevant Android and Google policies.

5. App Lock and Biometrics

Worthly may use an optional four-digit app passcode and Android biometric authentication, such as fingerprint authentication, to restrict access to the application.

Worthly does not receive or store your fingerprint or other biometric data. Biometric authentication is handled by the Android operating system.

6. Notifications

Worthly can create local reminders for scheduled EMI payments. Notifications are generated locally by the application.

To reduce exposure of sensitive financial information, notification content is designed not to display liability names or payment amounts on the lock screen.

7. Android Backup and Device Services

Android devices may provide operating-system backup or device-transfer services. Worthly does not operate those services and does not control how Android handles data that is eligible for such system services.

Worthly's security configuration is designed to exclude its primary financial-record database and secure app-lock credentials from the configured Android backup rules. Users should also consider their device-level backup and security settings when storing sensitive financial information.

8. Data Deletion

Worthly provides functionality to clear financial records from the application. You can also remove application data through Android's device settings or uninstall the application.

Because Worthly does not maintain a remote financial database, there is no Worthly cloud account from which your financial records need to be separately deleted.

9. Children's Privacy

Worthly is a general personal finance application and is not directed specifically toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Worthly account or online service.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Worthly's functionality or privacy practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
